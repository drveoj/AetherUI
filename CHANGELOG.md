# Lattice

## 2.0.2 - 2026-10-14

- Weapon enchants show again: Rockbiter, poisons, sharpening stones, oils and fishing lures each appear as a square at the right-hand end of your buff row, one per weapon. Hover it for the weapon's tooltip, which names the enchant and how long is left.
- On WoW Forever, a Focus frame. Use /focus, or Set Focus from a unit's right-click menu, and a smaller frame in the target's look appears below and to the right of your target, with its buffs, debuffs and casts. Switch it off or resize it under Settings > Unit frames.
- In unlock mode, a frame dropped on top of another can be picked up again. The smaller one sits on top, and Alt-click sends one behind so you can reach the one underneath.
- Opening a chest, a node or a door reads OPENING on the cast bar, not "OPENING - NO TEXT".
- Error reports (/lattice errors) list your other addons and their versions, so a report says what else was running.
- Fixed: in the Toolbox's Addons branch, some addons showed their folder name instead of their own name.

## 2.0.1 - 2026-10-12

- The Toolbox can sit on the right. Pick Left or Right under Settings > Toolbox, or in unlock mode drag the Lattice glyph at the top of the Toolbox to the other half of the screen. The minimap and the World trunk move to the other side. While you drag, the screen shows where each one will land.
- The side is saved with your profile, so every character on it shares it, and a layout you copy or paste keeps it.
- On WoW Forever, the Toolbox Menu has the same buttons as the game's own menu, in the same order. A button the game greys out is greyed out here too, and says why.
- Buttons look the same everywhere. The main action is filled, the others are outlined, and on/off switches are the diamond from the settings window.
- Enter presses the main button in the tour, the bank bag confirmation and the layout window.
- Panel titles are all one size, and the settings, layout and error windows have the same border as the rest.
- Every piece of text a player sees can now be translated.
- Fixed on WoW Forever: opening Edit Mode from the game menu showed errors, and dragging a frame in Edit Mode near Lattice's frames could show hundreds of them. The frames Lattice replaces no longer appear in Edit Mode.
- Fixed on WoW Forever: the preview under Settings > Unit frames showed an error.
- Fixed: picking a countdown time in the Party branch did nothing and closed the branch.
- Fixed: some addons' minimap buttons (SmartBuff, for one) came back on the minimap instead of staying in the Toolbox's Addons branch.
- Fixed: after a full restart of the game, some button labels could come up blank until a /reload.

## 2.0.0 - 2026-10-11

- AetherUI is now called Lattice. Same addon, new name. Your settings start fresh, and /lattice tour sets everything up again in about a minute.
- Full support for WoW Forever as it launches on November 4th, alongside Classic Era.
- The flight console now works on WoW Forever, including the new routes in Riverglades and Mount Hyjal.
- A new first-run tour. Your screen starts with just your own frame, and each stop adds the next piece and asks one thing about it. Run it again from Settings > Take the tour.
- The settings window is a map of your own screen: point at the thing you want to change, or search with /.
- Frames hang from each other now: the target from the player, your bars from the line between you and your target. Move one and what hangs from it comes too.
- Unlock mode shows how things connect: a dot field to snap to, the lines between frames, and an inspector. Drop a frame on another's junction to hang it from that one.
- Every action bar can be reshaped from its corner in unlock: any number of rows or columns, uneven shapes, wrapping down instead of across. Drag a bar's edge against another's to join them into one block.
- Each bar has its own opacity at rest and in a fight, can show only on hover, and can show its keybinds on its buttons. Hide a bar by dragging it to the row along the bottom in unlock; click it there to bring it back.
- Your whole layout is one line of text you can copy, share and paste back. Three starting layouts come with it: Rows, Block and Split.
- The quest tracker is now the World trunk under the minimap: your quests with their item buttons, and Mail, Tracking, Calendar and the music player as nodes. In a fight it draws up out of the way and leaves your active quest showing.
- On Classic Era, the Tracking node lists the tracking spells you know - click one to cast it - and wears the one that is on.
- The Toolbox is now the left trunk: Menu, Widgets, Addons, Settings, What's new, and Party while you're in a group.
- A branch off either trunk closes when you click anywhere else, like the game's own menus.
- Eight new skins: Amethyst, Sapphire, Emerald and Ruby, and Winter, Spring, Summer and Autumn, alongside Midnight, Dawn, Noon and Dusk. Pick one under Theme or in the tour.
- Cast bars are lanes on the line between you and your target, and breath and fatigue run along it too. Combo points and other class resources sit in a tray under your frame.
- Shields such as Power Word: Shield now show on your unit and party health bars, with a switch and a colour picker under Unit frames.
- On WoW Forever, an extra action button that appears mid-fight now shows. The taxi or vehicle leave button and the extra action share one slot.
- /lattice is the command now. /aether still works for this version.
- Fixed: with the game's chat timestamps on, "Keep Blizzard's [1. General]" switched off did nothing. The channel name now comes off as it should.
- Fixed: several settings descriptions showed a stray "\n\n" instead of a paragraph break.
- Fixed: scrolling the minimap past its furthest zoom out showed an error on Classic Era.
- Fixed: switching off the stance or extra bar did not stick; they came back on a form change or a taxi.
- Fixed: unlocking frames during a fight caused "action blocked" errors. It is now refused until the fight ends.
- Fixed: switching unit frames off left an invisible patch where your frames were that still targeted on a click.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-05

- AetherUI now runs on the WoW Forever beta as well as Classic Era, and will support the full release when it launches on November 4th.
- AetherUI no longer restyles the game's own windows. The character sheet, spellbook, talents, vendors, mail and the rest are back to Blizzard's look. The work now goes into the HUD, bags, quest log, Toolbox, Zen and the flight console.
- The flight console isn't available on WoW Forever yet.
- Settings have a window of their own. Options > AddOns > AetherUI has a button that opens it.
- Action bars now use the game's own button size. If you never picked a slot size yourself, yours moves with it; one you chose is kept.
- The quest tracker no longer empties itself when a zone is folded in the quest log, and says so when quests are hidden behind one.
- Scrolling a list no longer throws an error.
- The Talents button is hidden below level 10 rather than offered and doing nothing, and appears the moment you reach it.
- Menu glyphs shrink with their row instead of overlapping each other on a narrow dock.
- Right-clicking the minimap cancels your tracking again.
- Turning a module off and on again puts its look back properly, rather than leaving Blizzard's art on top of ours.
- Numpad keybinds read as symbols - the numpad plus shows as N+ rather than NPLUS.
- Bug reports now carry the interface version they were taken on.
